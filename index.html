FoodTrax

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

FoodTrax is a grocery list, budget and calorie tracking app for Android, made by Software-98. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This policy says what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, and why.

What stays on your phone

  • Your lists and kits, items, prices, stores, trips, budget and settings.
  • The calorie tracker, if you turn it on: your profile (sex, birth year, height, weight, activity, pregnancy or breastfeeding), your goal, the food you log, your own foods and your weigh-ins.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We never see it, and the app never sends it anywhere, except as described below when you choose to share a list or make a backup.

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup. That is Google's service, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Scanning a barcode. To find a product's name or nutrition, the app sends the barcode number, and nothing else, to Open Food Facts, a free public food database.
  • Sharing a list live. The phones find each other through the PeerJS service, which passes the share code between them, and then connect directly to each other, encrypted. The list's name, store and items go to the phones that join with your code. Nothing is stored on a server.
  • Sending a copy of a list. The list is inside the QR code or text code you show or send. Only someone you give it to can read it.
  • Backups. A backup file holds all of your data, including the calorie tracker. It is saved where you choose, such as your phone or a cloud drive, and is then in your care.
  • Ads. Unless you buy Remove ads, the app shows up to three full-screen ads a day through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which it can estimate an approximate location), and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks first, and you can change your choice in the app's Settings, under Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.
  • Remove ads. The purchase is made through Google Play, which handles the payment. The app only learns whether you own Remove ads.

The camera is used only to read barcodes and QR codes, on your phone. Pictures are not saved or sent.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, build profiles of you, or share your lists or health information with anyone. The app does not use your phone's location, and it does not send you emails, texts or notifications.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all data removes everything the app keeps on your phone. Stop tracking, on the calorie tracker's Profile tab, deletes the calorie data alone. Uninstalling the app also deletes it all. As we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server; for ad data, see Google's choices linked above.

Children

FoodTrax is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
